# HA Growatt 0.2.0

The app now restores the last readings after a quiet overnight restart. Their
original timestamps are kept, and controls remain unavailable until the
datalogger reconnects and its settings can be read.

If the Growatt cloud connection fails, the proxy can answer the datalogger
locally so readings continue. Connection and diagnostic entities, supported
inverter settings and refresh/time-sync buttons are also included.

Open the app's web page for connection checks, per-inverter profiles, downloadable
redacted diagnostics and a preview of existing entity/history mappings and
Energy Dashboard compatibility. New installations can obtain MQTT settings
from Home Assistant automatically. Explicit broker settings are preserved.

SPH/SPA charging and discharging periods can be edited in the app or through HA
text and switch entities. A period is written as one group and read back before
success is reported. Additional MIN TL-XH and MOD/MID TL3-XH controls use their
own register profiles. These additions require an explicit matching profile and
experimental controls enabled; they are disabled by default.

Community issue reports helped exclude ineffective or disputed registers and
identify firmware and datalogger limitations. See the
[hardware evidence](../hardware-support.md) for model-specific sources.

## Checks completed on 20 September 2026

- 2,906 tests passed on Python 3.12, 3.13 and 3.14 on Windows, and against the
  installed Linux package. Lint, formatting and package builds passed.
- Images built and passed service, sniffer and app-entry checks on amd64,
  arm64, arm/v7 and 386.
- Native Home Assistant checked MQTT controls, grouped schedule entities,
  migration preview, diagnostics, reload and disconnected controls. Existing
  entity and history identities were retained.
- A separate cold Core/app restart with an empty broker restored readings with
  their original timestamps without a new inverter packet.
- A temporary Supervisor app verified automatic MQTT setup, profile persistence
  across restart, ingress access and read-only Core WebSocket permissions.
- The app page was checked on desktop and mobile, including downloads and a
  delayed schedule response arriving after the selected period changed.
- Read-only collection from the existing installation received both fresh feeds
  and all 64 existing discovery records. No household setting was changed.

The household has no Growatt battery inverter available for physical control
testing. Protocol tests and reports from other projects do not establish that
every firmware or datalogger will accept these controls. Newer session-key
encrypted datalogger traffic remains unsupported.

This release does not rename entities, transfer another integration's history,
change Energy Dashboard settings or upgrade an installed app automatically.
